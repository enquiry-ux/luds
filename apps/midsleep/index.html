Estimates from your own entries, not a medical device. The energy curve is a two-process model — homeostatic sleep pressure since waking, sleep inertia, the midafternoon trough and the evening wake-maintenance zone — scaled by your sleep debt. Circadian landmarks use the published offsets: melatonin onset about two hours before habitual sleep onset, temperature minimum about two hours before habitual wake. Chronotype uses the Munich ChronoType Questionnaire's sleep-corrected midsleep on free days. Habit effects are observed differences between your own nights, not proof of cause.
